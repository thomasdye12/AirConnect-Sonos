Input-session investigation — 14 October 2026

Scope: static analysis of Connect model 17 firmware 86.10-81100, not the exact installed 86.10-80260 and not the Play:3 model-specific binary. No live test succeeded in this follow-up.

The registration routine at 0x10605ff8 scans nine entries (12 bytes each) from object+0x2e0. It invokes the source initialization method at vtable+8. On success (zero), it stores type, handler, and context at entry offsets 0, 4, 8 (0x10606084 through 0x1060608c).
The constructor zeroes all nine entries at 0x10608df0 through 0x10608e08. The only direct branch-to-registration found in this executable is 0x101f749c, passing type 2 (Spotify) at 0x101f7498. A literal pointer search found no embedded pointer to the registration entry. This is evidence about direct calls, not proof against inlining or other indirect initialization paths.
The activation routine searches those entries at 0x10607070. If no matching entry exists, activation fails. A matching entry with a null handler instead follows a success branch; a non-null handler invokes its activation method. This distinction corrects the initial interpretation. Handler activation uses vtable+0x10. Failure propagates to 402 at 0x106072cc.
Thus the URI label (type 6) is not itself a registered external PCM input. There is no verified type-6 external-source handler in the examined Connect executable. The Play:3 may differ and needs runtime validation or its own binary inspection. The previous type-5 rejection cannot be attributed conclusively to this nearby Connect firmware.

With no selected entry it returns failure; a matching entry with a null handler is treated as success, whereas a non-null handler is called through vtable+0x10.
